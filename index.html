PROJECTS

Selected work

Jun 2026
Simulating Unsteady-State Heat Conduction & Thermal Bridging in Constrained Environments using 2D Cellular Automata

A custom 2D physics engine in Python that simulates unsteady-state heat transfer using cellular automata, visually modeling thermal bridging — heat escaping through structural weak points in insulated environments, such as a metal bolt passing through an insulated wall. The engine tracks the Fourier stability limit automatically and uses hodgepodge machine rules on a von Neumann neighborhood to provide a lightweight, accurate way to predict and visualize where and how fast energy loss occurs in a constrained space.
